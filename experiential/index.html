01 Bacardi House Party

A house party. Anywhere.

Timeline
2 months
Where
Dubai Marina Beach, summer 2016
Role
Creative director · space & 3D · interaction
Challenge
A mobile, modular house built in 1 month
Nothing in the way.

Bacardi's global House Party said "There is nothing in the way." With my team at You Experience Dubai, we built the house for real, moved it between locations, and filled it with custom games for the city's top influencers.

Around it: the #HousePartyDXB campaign on Facebook and Instagram, with videos and custom GIFs.

"Being with the people you love is the perfect start for any party."
