01 The brief

New company. Big story.

Story + Film = Scooch Over

One idea behind every decision: a bridge between the page and the screen.

01 / Problem

No track record

A brand-new company, about to pitch investors and platforms. It had to look established.

02 / Deadline

One month

A small budget and a hard launch date: an annual film festival in Argentina.

03 / Goal

Page meets screen

An identity that joins the world of books with the world of film.

02 Process

Sketch. Test. Repeat.

Film reels and cameras felt ordinary. The double O told the whole story.

03 The idea

Two rings. Three stories.

The two Os lock together. One shape, three meanings.
