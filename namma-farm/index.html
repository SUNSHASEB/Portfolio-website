Namma Farm

A South Indian food brand, rebuilt to stand out on shelves around the world.

01 The brief

Modernise without losing soul.

நம்ம

Namma means “ours” in Tamil. A brand that belongs to its community.

01 / Problem

Lost in the aisle

The old logo was busy and text-heavy. Like its competitors, it disappeared on the shelf.

02 / Audience

South Indians abroad

South Indians abroad come first. Curious food lovers looking for safe, honest food come second.

03 / Goal

Simple. Modern. Tamil.

A premium identity you can spot from 20 feet away, with its heritage still in it.

02 Inspiration

Temples, kolams & paddy fields.

Everything started from real South Indian life: rituals, food, farmers and folk art.

03 Two routes

Pencil first.

Direction 01

Initials + leaf

An “NF” monogram with a leaf for the farm. Clean, but it could belong to any brand.

Chosen
Direction 02

Heritage geometry

A mark drawn from kolam floor art. The name is written in Tamil and English.

Kolam, made modern

04 Construction

Drawn on a kolam grid.

One letterform, turned four times around a grid of dots, like the kolams drawn outside South Indian homes each morning. Watch it build, or drag through it.

It starts like a kolam: a grid of dots on the ground.
