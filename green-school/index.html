Green School

Brand guidelines for a global network of eco-schools, from the bamboo campus in Bali to new schools around the world.

01 The brief

One school. Many campuses.

Green School started in Bali. Now it is growing worldwide.

Each new campus brings its own audiences, stories and imagery. The guidelines give every school the same core DNA, with room to tell local stories.

The toolkit covers brand architecture, the logo system, colour, type, tone, photography, social media and merchandise.

02 Brand DNA

Built on what they believe.

Mission

To create a global community of learners making our world more sustainable.

03 Logo system

The heart of school.

The logo is a drawing of the Heart of School, the bamboo building at the centre of the Bali campus where it all began. One global mark, with rules for every school and sub-brand.

The default global logo.

Clear space so the mark can breathe.

Built to scale down to 100px.

Full colour first, reversed when needed.

What never to do with the mark.

Each campus adds its location, nothing more.

Sub-brands carry a Green School lock-up.

04 Colour & type

Fresh, modern, distinctive.

Two greens and a light brown lead every piece. Open Sans keeps type simple and readable, like the campus itself.

05 Photography

Real moments, natural light.

Moment-based photography: students engaged, learning in the open, never posed. Clear rules on what to avoid keep every school on-brand.

Capture a moment in the day of a learner.

Bold use of open space.

Natural daylight, no filters.

Show the campus as part of the story.

No filters, vignettes or heavy edits.

No static, blurry or over-dramatic shots.

06 Icon set

07 Social media

Made for the feed.

Post sizes, crop rules and themes for Instagram, with do and don’t examples so any school can post with confidence.

Every format and ratio, in one place.

Tight frames on a student’s face and hands.

The learning tool sits at the centre.

Shoot as a participant, not an observer.

What makes a post win.

Gifts that grow.

Natural materials only: kraft, bamboo and raw paper, laser-engraved with the mark.
