Two short, art-directed music videos. I made the pictures, wrote the music and cut them together.

2026
Role
Art direction, music, edit
Made with

Two vertical shorts, 9:16
01The films02Process
01 · The films

Two moods. Fifteen seconds each, or less.

Each one is a full idea: a look, a song and a cut. Tap a frame to jump, or play it with sound.

01Mein

Cold and close. Chrome skin, red light, faces folded into mirror images, then a sudden warm cut.

02Wajd

Warm and wild. A single dancer lost in the music. Wajd is the Sufi word for that state.

02 · Process

Pictures, sound, cut.

I directed both sides at once, so the picture and the music grew up together.

  1. 01Art directionAdobe Firefly · Codex

    Every look and every shot, generated and refined until it felt right.

  2. 02MusicSuno

    The songs, written for the pictures.

  3. 03Arrange & mixAbleton Live 12

    Cut to length, arranged and mixed.

  4. 04EditAfter Effects

    Cut to the beat, graded and finished.

Directed in picture and sound.
