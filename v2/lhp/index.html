Lighthouse

A sharper LHP: refining a multidisciplinary agency’s logo and identity, inspired by the precision of a katana blade.

Client
Lighthouse Productions (LHP)
Role
Creative & brand designer
Year
2023
Scope
Logo refinement, identity, guidelines
01The brief

Breathe new life into the mark.

  1. 01Refine, don’t replace

    Evolve the existing LHP logo instead of starting over.

  2. 02The legacy

    Honour the heritage, so it still feels familiar.

  3. 03A sharper edge

    A contemporary look that works across digital and physical.

02The logo

Sharpened like a blade.

The client referenced a katana. Instead of drawing a sword, the refined mark borrows its discipline: clean, precise lines, strong geometric balance and a sharper silhouette that stays legible at any size.

03Visual language

Cut from the blade.

Supporting shapes taken from the “blade section”: angled cuts, hazard stripes and HUD lines, in high-contrast black with lime and orange.

04Brand guidelines

A system to grow into.

  1. 01Logo rules

    Usage, clear space and minimum sizes.

  2. 02Colour

    Black and white, lime as signal, orange as heat.

  3. 03Typography

    A refined, consistent type system.

  4. 04Applications

    Apparel, vehicles, stationery and screens.

05In use

Built for the screen.

Reveal what was already there.
