What would Valorant look and feel like on a phone? A two-week concept for menus, agent select, HUD and motion. Not a port, a rethink.

01 · Overview

Why Valorant Mobile?

Desktop-only game. Massive mobile FPS audience.

02 · The problem

A precision game on an imprecise input.

  1. 01Dense HUD, small screen

    Health, armour, abilities, minimap, economy, kill feed and crosshair, with thumbs covering part of it.

  2. 02Keyboard → touch

    Move, aim, shoot, crouch, jump, four abilities, reload and swap, mapped to two thumbs.

  3. 03Competitive clarity

    Rounds are won in seconds. The key information has to read instantly, mid-fight.

  4. 04Brand fidelity

    Red, off-white and navy, angular geometry, confident type. Not a generic mobile shooter.

03 · Process

Two weeks, five phases.

  1. 01Research

    Days 1–3How mobile shooters handle controls and HUD density.

  2. 02Ideation

    Days 3–5Thumb-zone sketches and layout options.

  3. 03Accessibility

    Days 5–7Reach, contrast and tap sizes.

  4. 04UI

    Days 7–12Hi-fi screens and system in Figma.

  5. 05Motion

    Days 12–14Transitions and agent intros in After Effects.

04 · Brand guidelines

One brand, a new platform.

Logo versions for every size and use, plus a style guide that keeps type, colour and layout consistent across screens.

05 · Key design decision

The PC HUD, rebuilt for touch.

Don't shrink Valorant. Make a phone feel like home.
