01 · The brief

One home for two companies.

  1. 01No centre

    Two businesses needed one parent brand that speaks with one voice.

  2. 02Ministries & C-suite

    Senior Saudi leaders, and the UK universities who partner with them.

  3. 03Built for Vision 2030

    Feel part of the Kingdom’s transformation, not a generic overseas school.

02 · What APL is

A bridge, not a school.

APL is a Saudi-based professional learning and development consultancy. It connects Saudi ministries, corporations and institutions with world-class executive education from 250+ global universities.

03 · Process

Sketch. Explore. Refine.

Five routes explored. The triangle won.

04 · The idea

One peak. Two halves.

Arabic geometry, built from simple triangles.

  1. 01Apex: the peak of a triangle, for excellence
  2. 02The initials APL, hidden in the geometry
  3. 03Arabesque patterns, made modern and modular
