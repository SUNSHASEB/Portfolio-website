Brawl3rs

An AI-driven autobattler where meme-coin fighters brawl and players bet on the outcome. I built the brand and the full UI system in two months.

Role
Sole UI designer, team of 16
Scope
Brand, UI system, animation, social
Tools

01Overview

Meme coins, in the ring.

Gaming immersion. Trader-grade clarity.

02The problem

Four challenges, one deadline.

  1. 01A brand from zero

    No logo, no colours, no voice. Everything had to be created.

  2. 02Game meets trading floor

    Gamer immersion and trader-grade data, on the same screen.

  3. 03Dark, still readable

    An atmospheric theme that never costs legibility.

  4. 04Every screen size

    Desktop, tablet and mobile, each with its own navigation.

03The origin story

From Frogs vs Dogs to Brawl3rs.

It started as Frogs vs Dogs: Pepe versus Doge, two of crypto's biggest meme coins. As the roster and mechanics grew, the name had to grow too. The brand had to feel rooted in meme culture but scale to an unlimited roster.

⇄Frogs vs DogsBrawl3rs
04Key design decisions

Five calls that shaped it.

  1. 01Dark is the brand

    Not a mode, the identity. Every colour, contrast and glow was made for dark backgrounds.

  2. 02Neon green

    "Go" in games, "profit" in trading. One colour that speaks both languages.

  3. 03Activity as social proof

    A live stream and chat show what others bet, win and react to.

  4. 04Progressive complexity

    Degen Mode and Turbo stay hidden until players go deeper.

  5. 05Component-first

    A system, not screens, so 16 people could build from day one.

05UI system

One system, built for the dark.

Buttons, cards, panels, navigation, modals, forms, tables, charts and status indicators. Every screen at desktop, tablet and mobile.

  • Fight Roster
  • Live Stream
  • Derivatives Market
  • Leaderboards
  • Rankings
  • Profile & Quests
  • Cashier
  • Turbo Mode
  • Degen Mode
  • Onboarding
  • Activity Stream & Chat
06Social & community

Built to launch a community.

Twitter and Discord visuals: profile images, banners, announcement templates, stickers and promo graphics, ready before launch day.

07Outcome & reflection

Shipped in two months.

Launched in 2023. Still live at brawl3rs.ai.
