NRJ

Brand systems

A premium energy drink built on “pure potential”: power, clarity and modern performance, without the category’s visual noise.

01 · The brief

Energy, without the noise.

  1. 01Stand apart

    A premium, minimal identity in a loud, aggressive category.

  2. 02One clear idea

    A concept-led system, not decoration.

  3. 03Built to scale

    Room for many flavours and future SKUs.

02 · Research

Break the category pattern.

Most energy drinks lean on the same cues. NRJ speaks to a more design-conscious, premium buyer.

  • Metallic gradients
  • Explosive graphics
  • High-contrast textures
  • Hyper-masculine cues
03 · The concept

Inspired by the battery.

The universal symbol of stored power. Its polarity, positive and negative, shapes the logo, the layout and the packaging.

04 · Logo system

Charged and directional.

A mark you can recognise at a glance, from the can to the shelf.

  1. 01Polarity

    Plus and minus symbols, straight from the battery.

  2. 02Geometry

    Minimal, geometric forms.

  3. 03Type

    A clean, modern typographic structure.

Sketchbook · 8 ideas
  1. BatteryA
  2. PolarityA
  3. Charge level
  4. Bolt
  5. Speed linesB
  6. Arrow
  7. MotionB
  8. Power

Narrowed to two directions

05 · Packaging

Power through simplicity.

A strict, minimal hierarchy that stays uncluttered on shelf.

  1. 01Vertical logotype

    Bold and unmistakable.

  2. 02Polarity accents

    Marks that echo the battery.

  3. 03Flavour colour

    High-contrast identifiers.

  4. 04Clean info

    An ordered, readable layout.

06 · Flavours & SKUs

One layout. Every flavour.

Each variant gets its own accent colour inside the same structure, so the range blocks well on shelf and new SKUs slot straight in.

07 · At retail

Built for the shelf.

Multipacks, a counter display and a floor stand carry the same system.

08 · Visualisation

Built in Figma Weave.

One node workflow for the hero shot: freeze the can in ice, shatter the block, release the charge. The same graph makes the stills and the film.

Power through clarity, not chaos.
