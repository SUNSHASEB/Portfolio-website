Scooch Over

Brand systems

A new film company, bringing a famous book to the screen. It needed to look credible from day one.

Role
Sole designer
Timeline
1 month
Scope
Logo, identity, guidelines, digital
Markets
Canada · Argentina
01The brief02Process03The idea04The identity05In the world06Photography
01 · The brief

New company. Big story.

  1. 01No track record

    A brand-new company, about to pitch investors and platforms. It had to look established.

  2. 02One month

    A small budget and a hard launch date: an annual film festival in Argentina.

  3. 03Page meets screen

    An identity that joins the world of books with the world of film.

02 · Process

Sketch. Test. Repeat.

Film reels and cameras felt ordinary. The double O told the whole story.

03 · The idea

Two rings. Three stories.

The two Os lock together. One shape, three meanings.

  1. 01Film reels, passing the story from one to the next
  2. 02Two founders, Sergio and Romina, working as one
  3. 03Story and film, locked into a single frame

05 · In the world

Ideas in. Stories out.

The website and social channels launched in time for the festival.

06 · Photography

Shot like a film still.

Moody light, bold colour, silhouettes. Feeling over faces.

The story already exists. We’re here to bring it to life.
