01 · Brief

Get Bali back outdoors.

Lockdown kept everyone inside. Sthala wanted people living in Bali, and travellers stuck on the island, to see that its riverside grounds are made for yoga, and to bring in guests through Bali’s own yoga teachers.

  • Awareness
  • Footfall
  • Yoga influencers
  • Open-air classes
02 · Audience

Two ways in.

Two calls to action, one for each kind of guest.

Singles

People living in Bali, or stuck here while travel is closed, who want to practise yoga in the open air.

  • Riverside classes
  • Moonlight yoga
  • Juice bar
Families

Parents in Bali can bring their kids to class and use the hotel’s new kids’ play room and infinity pool.

  • Kids’ play room
  • Infinity pool
  • Family classes

06 · Meet the teacher

Meet your teacher.

Short films introduce each teacher: how they teach, and why they practise in nature. Press play, sound on.

Voice-over
  1. For me yoga is my connection to the earth.
  2. The sound of the water flowing gently past.
  3. The feel of the warm sun as I reach towards the sky.
  4. In that moment I am one with nature.
  5. I transcend the ordinary.
  6. By going inside, I change the outside.
  7. With each breath, the mind, body and soul are revitalised.
  8. And everything feels right. Just right.
