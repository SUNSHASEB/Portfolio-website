01 Background

Hired by TLM to fix the UI.

UMM is the tool Coca-Cola uses to measure every kind of media on one scale. It was built by The Coca-Cola Company, Top Line Marketing and Kantar.

Who built UMM
  • The Coca-Cola CompanyThe client and the users: marketing teams in every market.
  • Top Line Marketing (TLM)Created the UMM framework. Hired me to fix the UI.
  • KantarAdded its creative and media effectiveness methods.
  • Sherlock AIThe data engine underneath, by Infinite Analytics.
My brief

The model worked. The interface fought it.

TLM had a powerful measurement tool that marketers found hard to read and hard to steer. My job: make the UI as clear as the idea behind it.

One currency for all media

UMM puts paid, owned, earned and shared media on one scale. Every touchpoint gets a quality score, so a TV ad and a Coke bottle can be compared. Pick one.

    04 Approach

    Five moves.

    Mapped every screen, old to new, and ranked each issue by severity.

    Guiding principleConsistency, context and scalability, before decoration.

    05 Heuristic audit

    Ranked by severity.

    Every screen checked against usability heuristics. Fix the critical ones first.

    Ranked by severity
    HeuristicIssue foundSeverityFix
    Visual consistencyLight and dark themes mixed across flowsCriticalOne dark design system, end to end
    Efficiency of useChanging scope meant opening a pop-upCriticalFilter rail, always visible
    Match to scaleFlat checklists for markets and touchpointsHighSearchable, nested, multi-select pickers
    VisibilityTouchpoint filter not on screenHighTouchpoint filter added to the rail
    RecognitionCharts had no legends or deltasModerateLegends, sparklines and change vs. last year
    DiscoverabilityPlanning tools buried and vaguely namedModerateA named planning suite, front and centre
