Tamuda Bay

A logo and launch campaign for a coastal development in Tetouan, Morocco. Moorish tiles meet the Mediterranean.

Role
Logo, brand DNA & campaign
Partner
With Shahrukh Mirza
Year
2018
Place
Tetouan, Morocco

01 The brief

Where two worlds meet.

01 / Place

Tetouan coast

Commercial, retail and residential developments on a vast Moroccan beach.

02 / Roots

Moorish heritage

An identity that honours Morocco’s history and architecture.

03 / Feel

Modern luxury

Clean, timeless and contemporary. Nothing hard, sharp or rigid.

02 Inspiration

From a tile to a line.

Moorish tiles are everywhere in Morocco. I simplified their complex patterns down to a circle and a line: the building blocks of the Tamuda Bay DNA.

01Start with the tile

Rounded Moorish patterns and arabesque set the rules for every shape.

02Strip it back

Remove detail until only the circle and its dividing lines remain.

03Use it everywhere

One graphic device that frames headlines across every channel.

03 The logo

Waves and a modern line.

The mark comes from the beach around Tetouan: curved stripes for waves, and a thin, modern wordmark beside them. Watch it come together.

04 The campaign

Two elements. One perfect place.

Every poster joins two worlds in one silhouette. The circle from the brand DNA frames each headline.

Billboards carry the same circle into the landscape.

Retreat into nature’s treat.

Enter the lifestyle of your dreams.

Get away from the everyday.
