ORO Chocolate

A golden identity for Nicaraguan chocolate and coffee, grown from the star inside a cacao pod.

Role

Timeline

01 The brief

Two categories. One gold.

01 / Problem

No voice

A premium, ethically sourced product with no visual language to carry its story.

02 / Challenge

Coffee + cacao

One identity that works for single-origin coffee and artisan chocolate.

03 / Goal

Honest origin

Show Nicaragua's volcanic soil, rainforest and craft, without borrowing clichés.

I led identity, guidelines and packaging.Echo Digital led the brief, digital, campaigns and go-to-market.
02 The idea

Cut from cacao.

01ORO means gold

The name made the palette obvious. Gold leads, everything else supports it.

02Look inside the pod

A cacao pod's cross-section hides a star. That shape started the sketches.

03Keep it geometric

Clean lines and simple shapes turned the star into a mark that works tiny or huge.

03 Brand guidelines

One book. Every team.

Strong enough to onboard any future agency or in-house team without watering the brand down.

Contents: framework, brand world, packaging and more.

The big idea: Reflective Indulgence.

Gold first, with black and grey to hold it.

One mark, two lines: Chocolate and Coffee.
