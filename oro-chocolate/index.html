01 The brief

Two categories. One gold.

01 / Problem

No voice

A premium, ethically sourced product with no visual language to carry its story.

02 / Challenge

Coffee + cacao

One identity that works for single-origin coffee and artisan chocolate.

03 / Goal

Honest origin

Show Nicaragua's volcanic soil, rainforest and craft, without borrowing clichés.

I led identity, guidelines and packaging.Echo Digital led the brief, digital, campaigns and go-to-market.
02 The idea

Cut from cacao.

01ORO means gold

The name made the palette obvious. Gold leads, everything else supports it.

02Look inside the pod

A cacao pod's cross-section hides a star. That shape started the sketches.

03Keep it geometric

Clean lines and simple shapes turned the star into a mark that works tiny or huge.

03 Brand strategy

Reflective indulgence.

Purpose, story, customer and voice, all built on one truth: chocolate and coffee you can trace back to the farm.

Brand essence

Purpose, story, personality and the mind map boil down to three values. Tap each one, then sum them up.

Tone of voice

Warm, welcoming and passionate about the craft. Tap a word.

To traceably curate artisanal chocolate, sourced and crafted in Nicaragua.

The cacao and coffee supply chains are broken. ORO is the traceable alternative.

What customers should think, feel and say about ORO.

Eco-conscious explorers of life’s pleasures, especially the chocolate kind.

A Chicago designer who seeks out premium, sustainable chocolate.

A marketing manager who wants great coffee that fits a fast life.

Mapped on a semantic differential graph, then an identity prism.

Quality ingredients, sustainability, fairness, innovation and community.
