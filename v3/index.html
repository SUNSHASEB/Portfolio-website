The short story

An art director's eye, behind the camera

I'm Sunil. For more than a decade I've art-directed campaigns and TV commercials: casting, storyboarding, directing models and crews in the studio and on location, then carrying the work through edit, grade and motion.

Away from set I carry a camera for myself. Strangers, streets, mountains, quiet light. No posing, just waiting for the frame.

I haven't filmed a wedding yet. This page shows what I would bring to one.

What I'd bring

Ease with people

I've directed models, casts and first-timers. I know how to settle someone who is nervous in front of a lens, and when to step back and let a moment happen.

Calm on a tight schedule

Commercial sets run to the minute with no second take on the light. I plan the shot list, read the room and keep moving without rushing anyone.

Stills and film, one eye

I think in sequences: the wide, the detail, the reaction. Storyboard to final grade, I already know what the edit will need while I'm shooting.
