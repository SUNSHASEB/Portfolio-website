01 Martini · Begin Desire

A global Italian platform, rebuilt for two African cities.

Client
MARTINI® · Bacardi Limited
My role
Art director: style, shoot and system
Deliverables
Key visuals, OOH, print, digital, templates, guidelines
Markets
Nigeria & Angola

Made in Lagos and Luanda. Not shipped to them.

In October 2014 MARTINI launched Begin Desire, a global platform built around a Jake Scott short film shot in Rome and the brand's "bar and ball" motif. Unmistakably Italian.

My brief was to take it into Nigeria and Angola, two markets with their own nightlife, visual languages and artists. I built the bridge: style direction, the shoot, the effect system and the guidelines local teams ran on after I stepped back.

Global consistency, no cultural flattening.

Push too far toward the master brand and the work reads as foreign. Push too far local and you lose the equity the global campaign was built to carry.

01Two markets, two cultures

Different languages, music scenes and design references. One template for both would fail both.

02Artists, not decoration

Leading local artists co-created the work, so the art direction had to leave them real room.

03Survive my absence

Local teams would make the long tail of retail, social and print. Everything had to be reproducible.

Two marketsSame system. Two different campaigns.

Nigeria

Built with the Lagos team and Nigerian artists already native to the city's nightlife and music.

  • On-location shoot with local talent
  • Artists' work in the illustrative layer
  • Key visuals across OOH, print, retail and digital
Angola

Developed with the Luanda team and Angolan artists: the same structure, tuned to a local palette and rhythm.

  • Market-specific casting and shoot
  • Independent artist take on the overlay
  • Full adaptation kit for local production

The art direction systemFour rules, and a lot of room.

Anything I could specify tightly, I did, so everything I couldn't specify was free.

01Photographic treatment

A fixed stack of grain, contrast, edge decay and colour bias, supplied as layered templates.

02Illustrative overlay

Bold, high-contrast and hand-led. Defined by behaviour, so local artists did the mark-making.

03Typographic command

BEGIN DESIRE holds the same position in every execution. Surface treatment is free.

04Palette discipline

MARTINI red anchors every frame. Everything else negotiates around it.

Portraits, type and the red ball, locked in one grid.

The overlay system flexes for each moment.

Cocktail and lifestyle formats share one structure.

Cream, red and black: the palette never moves.

The outcomeTwo campaigns that belonged where they ran.

Each market launched work that read as locally made while sitting clearly inside Begin Desire. The artists' work was load-bearing, not a badge, and the templates kept local teams producing on-brand assets long after launch. The grunge-plus-illustration treatment gave MARTINI a texture that felt like the walls, posters and nightlife of the cities it ran in.

What I took from it

Lock the few things that genuinely can't move, and you can hand over everything else. It's how I've approached multi-market art direction ever since.

03 Sprite · Pakistan

Spice, street cricket and thirst.

Three Sprite campaigns for Pakistan: a food-led brand platform, a nationwide hunt for street cricket talent, and a thirst campaign built on ice-cold lemon-lime.

Sprite & spiceMirchi Pe Lagao, Sprite Ka Tarka

Sprite with spicy food, launching the brand platform in Pakistan for the first time. I created the logo stamp that let the campaign own its space, and the campaign ID and visuals were later used in China and Mexico.

Sprite Cricket Next · 2012Team Hosh vs Team Josh

An activation-led campaign that set cricket greats Moin Khan (Team Hosh, the cool head) and Shoaib Akhtar (Team Josh, the fire) against each other to find Pakistan's best street cricketers for a nationwide tournament. Fans entered by SMS, the tournament came to their gali, and The Coca-Cola Company later used the campaign in the UAE.

ThirstTu Hai Pyaas Ka Dushman

You are thirst's enemy: crisp lemon-lime, ice and splash, across posters and wide-format banners.

04 Fanta · Pakistan

More Fanta, less serious.

Three Fanta campaigns for Pakistan in 2012, built on the brand's animated global crew and its "More Fanta, Less Serious" platform, and made local through games, music and school break time.

ActivationFanta Crazy Chase

Pakistan's biggest chase: a nationwide game to hunt down Fanta through sets of challenges, each one leading to the next clue. The global Fanta crew brought it to life on bridges, billboards, kiosks and stages.

Music activationFanta Rocks, with Noori

A music activation for tweens with Lahore rock band Noori: "Rock your school with Noori." The band steps into the animated Fanta world, kept firmly inside the brand guidelines.

Fanta Great Taste · 2012De Breaks Ke Mazay

Visuals built on Fanta’s snacking credentials and owning break time at school, localising the global Fanta animation so it felt made for Pakistan.

05 Coca-Cola · Pakistan

Cricket fever and Coke Studio.

T20 cricketHum Hain Opener Apni Zindagi Ke

We are the openers of our own lives. Young fans with bats, horns and green-and-white bunting, cheering the T20 season in Coca-Cola red.

Coke Studio · Season 5 · 2012A mandala for a music universe

Coke Studio is Coca-Cola Pakistan’s fusion music platform, pairing traditional sound with contemporary. Season 5 ran from 13 May to 8 July 2012, produced by Rohail and Umber Hyatt. I built the outdoor identity on the mandala, a spiritual symbol in Hinduism and Buddhism that represents the universe.
